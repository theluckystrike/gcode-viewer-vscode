# Layers and playback

- Move the "Layer" slider, or press Up and Down, to step through the print. "One layer" shows only the current layer.
- "Go to line" opens the G-code text beside the preview at the first line of that layer.
- Click a line in the 3D view to see its G-code line, feature, speed and layer, then jump to it.
- "Play" draws the print move by move with a nozzle marker. Pick 1× to 5000× speed, or drag the progress bar.
- "Color" switches between feature, speed and layer colors. "Travel" shows the moves where nothing is printed.
